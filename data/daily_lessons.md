LESSONS (updated 2026-10-14)

Evidence base so far is Cole's own rated takes; the daily experiments have produced only one take (43199a6e, Interstellar pipe organ, unrated as of today), so nothing from the daily log has taught anything yet.

What the 3-star takes share (a38b5c83, eec20d97, 324a1f7b, 1af58c0f, 16a924b4, 10919aaa, 94b60fc9 — all 3 stars):
1. A famous world AND a named, picturable moment: Calypso's shore, dawn over Arrakis, Penelope's loom, the ship running home, the sirens' strait. No abstract settings.
2. One soft, distant pulse only — frame drum "like a distant heartbeat", muffled oars, loom shuttle. Never a foreground beat.
3. One recognizable hook, named and described in intervals: a three-note descending vocal call, a four-note lyre figure, flute "homeward phrases".
4. A written journey: sparse opening (pedal + pulse + hook instrument), gradual gathering, fullest and CLOSER/WARMER past the midpoint, then a calm glow with everything still sounding through the final seconds. "No fade-out, no silence" appears in two 3-star prompts.
5. Specific harmony: a named mode/key, a BPM in the 40–64 range, a slow progression over a steady tonic pedal.
6. Diegetic scene sound kept quiet and distant: rope and hull creaks, waves at the bow, sand on the wind, loom creak.
7. Bans only for real problems heard before (no cymbals, no electric guitar, water stays distant).

What separates 2-star from 3-star (direct comparison available):
- Sirens 3-star (eec20d97) vs its 2-star sibling b043fc9e: the ONLY difference is that the 2-star version added "the peak is intimacy, not volume", "dissonance never grows beyond the opening", "the final quarter returns fully to the calm of the beginning". Neutering the arc cost a star. Lesson: let the middle really bloom; do not promise to return to the opening.
- 46368573 / 363eafe7 / dbc62b38 (all 2 stars) are the same scene written WITHOUT named parts (THE LURE / THE PERIL / THE SHIP) — one undifferentiated paragraph of instrument list. The 3-star version has the named parts. Lesson: named parts with jobs beat a flat instrument list, at least for busier scenes. (Exception: the Calypso and Dawn 3-stars are flowing single paragraphs — that form works when the palette is small and the mood is one thing.)
- 54ebbee0 (1 star) is a "dusk on the wine-dark sea" mood piece with no specific moment, no hook, and no written arc — just adjectives. Lesson: atmosphere without a moment, hook, or journey is the failure mode.

Holdings for the channel's rules: percussion-heavy is bad (Three Knocks v2.5 overshot); nothing dark under a hopeful story (Tau Ceti Vigil); plain titles.
