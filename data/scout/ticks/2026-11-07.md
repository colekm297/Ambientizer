# Scout tick 2026-11-07 (tick 5)
**Followed:** lead 1, can a franchise hold without its soundtrack? Audited Ambient Worlds' Skyrim 0.84 and Shire 0.60, walked its 220 long uploads and read credits. About 270 of 1,000 quota units.
**Main find:** the Skyrim question was the wrong test. The 0.84 is Jeremy Soule's soundtrack, on an upload only 559 days old; older Skyrim uploads score 0.21 and 0.14. It reads real, but it's mostly youth.
**Better test, The Shire:** the channel moved to human-made covers of Howard Shore's themes, and the world kept its premium: 5.43x and 5.13x the year's median on 2018-19 uploads, then 8.61x, 5.88x, 7.68x (2025) and 4.74x (2026). New recordings transferred. The melodies stayed, so this doesn't show a world carrying music with no themes. Credits since 2025-07 show no such upload, with Hocus Pocus and Disney parks too vague to say; older uploads unread.
**Lasting works at the world level:** old Shire uploads are 0.09 and 0.02 while new ones reach 2.7M to 3.1M. Demand moves to the newest version. One-upload scores understate worlds that get remade, which bears on our Odyssey and Dune series.
**Two rough controls:** ambience-only Skyrim on its second channel 2,709 views vs 2,509,011 (subs differ 26x, views 926x, confounded). No-franchise uploads ran 0.08x to 0.35x of median, and the nature ones drop the music as well.
**Relevant to the human-made idea:** Ambient Worlds now puts "Human Made" and "No AI" in titles and credits. Whether its covers are licensed isn't established, so this is no precedent for us.
**Dated event:** The Elder Scrolls VI: Sentinel, reported revealed 2026-11-03, set in Hammerfell with a desert teaser, no release date. No lasting evidence; the 0.84 predates it.
